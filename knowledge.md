### SB — Dual-spring slide balancer (Slides & undercuts)
Cancels the slide's weight so the design can ignore gravity. For slides that travel vertically on the press. Two opposed die springs in a sealed cartridge hold the slide at every point of its stroke, so it neither drops when the mold opens nor loads the angle pin on closing. It replaces a counterweight with a part that bolts into a standard pocket.
Problem: A heavy slide mounted on the top or bottom of the mold sags under its own weight as soon as the angle pin leaves it. The slide drifts, the retainer wears, and the angle pin takes a side load on every close. The usual fix is a one-off counterweight or an oversized spring plunger tuned by trial.
Solution: Two die springs act on the slide from opposite sides through a floating rod. Their preload is set so the net force equals the slide's weight at mid-stroke and stays within ±5 % over the whole travel. The slide behaves as if it had no mass in the gravity direction; the retainer and the angle pin see only the molding loads they were sized for.
Lead: stock, ships in 5 days. Options: Size = S/M/L/XL; Stroke = 20/40/60/80/100/120 mm. Example part number SB-S-20 at $108.00.
Specs: Housing: SKD61, 50–54 HRC; Springs: 2 × CrSi die springs, ISO 10243; Rod: SUJ2, 58–62 HRC, ground; Slide mass: S 2–6 · M 5–15 · L 12–35 · XL 30–80 kg; Stroke: 20 – 120 mm; Balance error: ≤ ±5 % of slide weight over stroke; Temperature: up to 200 °C; Rated life: 1 × 10⁶ cycles; Mounting: 4 × M6 (S/M) · 4 × M8 (L/XL), from slide back

Q: Which slide balancer size do I need? (滑块平衡器怎么选型号？)
A: By slide mass: S 2–6 kg, M 5–15 kg, L 12–35 kg, XL 30–80 kg. If the slide travels at an angle to vertical, use only the weight component along the travel (mass × cos angle). Tell me the mass, for example "18 kg", and I will pick the size. Above 80 kg we fit two XL units.
中文: 按滑块质量选：S 2–6 kg，M 5–15 kg，L 12–35 kg，XL 30–80 kg。滑块行程与竖直方向有夹角时，只算沿行程方向的重力分量（质量 × cos 角度）。直接告诉我质量，比如「18 kg」，我来选型。超过 80 kg 用两个 XL。
